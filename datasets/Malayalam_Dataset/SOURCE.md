# Malayalam Digit Dataset

From https://huggingface.co/datasets/anand-venugopal/malayalam-digits-dataset
(originally https://github.com/anandvenugopal-tech/malayalam-digit-dataset),
by Anand Venugopal, licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Included unchanged. At training time, src/train_script.py paints over the black
corners left by the rotated images; the files here are not modified.
