Updated changes to predict.py to solve accuracy loss for gallery images:

1. Image Inversion (ImageOps.invert):
Converted gallery images (black-on-white) to MNIST format (white-on-black).

2. Binarization/Thresholding (image.point):
Converted grayscale/noisy photos to pure binary black-and-white to remove shadows and lighting gradients.

3. Automatic Bounding Box Cropping (getbbox):
Added logic to detect the exact area containing the digit and crop out empty margins. This prevents the digit from becoming too small or off-center after resizing.

4. Proportional Scaling and Centering:
Instead of a forced resize to 28x28, the digit is now scaled to fit within a 20x20 area (maintaining aspect ratio) and then centered onto a 28x28 black background. This precisely mimics the MNIST dataset structure.

5. Simplified Dimensional Expansion:
Used np.expand_dims(image, axis=(0, -1)) to prepare the image for the CNN input shape (batch, height, width, channels).
